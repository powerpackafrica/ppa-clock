Staff never see these results and the card always works. Distance: smaller = more alike. "Closest other" is the most similar other enrolled person. Each result is also written to the Face check column of Clock Log (filter "Not match" and check the photo). Self-check: when a card is scanned we know who it is, so we test whether the face alone would have found the same person ("wrong" must stay 0). Face distance: only the biggest face is checked and smaller faces are ignored, so people queuing behind are not checked. Live test: green box = the face that would be checked, grey = ignored.

Face Recognition Consent

What we collect. The tablet takes a few photos of your face and turns them into a numeric face template. It cannot be turned back into a photo. At each clock in or out a small photo may also be saved with the time record.

Why. To confirm the card holder is the person clocking, to keep accurate attendance for payroll and leave, and to prevent clocking for another person.

Who can see it. Only HR and the General Manager. Data is stored in Power Pack Africa's company Google account and is not shared outside the company or used for any other purpose.

How long. Face template: while you work at Power Pack Africa. Clock-in photos: up to 12 months. Attendance records are kept as part of payroll records.

Your choice. Face recognition is optional and your card always works. You can withdraw consent at any time by telling HR, and your face template will be deleted.
